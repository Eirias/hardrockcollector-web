Fair play, built in.

Hard Rock Collector validates every check-in against the server. The app rejects you if you're more than 25 meters away. The server then re-computes the distance from your raw coordinates and rejects anything with GPS accuracy worse than 200 meters. You can't farm a café twice. You can't spoof from your couch. The leaderboard means something.

Privacy at a glance

  • Sign in with Apple only - no email lists, no passwords.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Location is read only when you tap check-in. No background tracking.
  • Delete your account in Settings - server data is wiped immediately.
